# Burlingame State Park Campground - Charlestown, Rhode Island

Sometimes the best camping traditions begin with one simple question: "Want to go check out that old cemetery?"

Our trip to Burlingame State Park Campground started with a missing tent pole and a trip to Walmart, but it turned into one of our favorite family camping adventures.

The campground is enormous, and one of its hidden treasures is the collection of historic Rhode Island cemeteries located right inside the park. Visiting Historic Cemetery CH058 sparked a family tradition that continues today. Now the young'uns actually look forward to adding historic cemeteries to our camping trips whenever we can.

The trip also included playful goats climbing the rocks, an unexpected encounter with an emu, a relaxing day at Misquamicut Beach, souvenir hunting, and evenings around the campfire.

## Trail Tater Take

Burlingame State Park remains one of our favorite destinations because it combines great camping with history, wildlife, and family memories.
