# Day 1 – Pointing Trail Tater Toward Plymouth

Wednesday, July 29, 2026

We arrived at Sandy Pond Campground around 2:10 PM after driving through several rounds of heavy rain. As we approached Plymouth the rain finally stopped, giving us a perfect window to set up camp.

Trail Tater was parked, leveled, and settled into its site. We pitched the family tents, arranged the campsite, and connected Starlink so we could post our first live Trail Tater journal update from Plymouth.

Dinner was burgers and hot dogs.

Tomorrow's breakfast will be pancakes, bacon, sausage, and plenty of maple syrup.

After camp was ready, we headed into Plymouth in search of food... just like our forefathers did when they first came here, only with GPS.

Day One is officially underway!
