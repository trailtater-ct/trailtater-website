# Trail Tater recipe style

Ken's standing direction, November 18, 2026: today's printed Trail Tater Cookbook Second Edition layout is the only recipe style to use from this day forward. Apply it to all new or revised recipe pages and printable recipe PDFs.

Use the November 18 holiday bread PDFs as the visual reference. Reuse their actual PDFs for downloads; do not reformat them.

- Black and white, US Letter portrait for PDFs.
- Thin double-line page frame with small hatched/wheat corner motifs.
- Centered TRAIL TATER COOKBOOK imprint with flanking rules.
- Large bold centered recipe title and italic subtitle.
- Makes / oven heat / total time line.
- Two side-by-side rounded ingredient / extra boxes, stacked on small screens.
- Numbered Trail Tater Method beneath the ingredients.
- Full-width rounded tip, variation, or make-ahead box.
- Italic footer: Outdoor Kitchen Recipes - camp favorites from the Trail Tater.

Keep the website's existing navigation and branding around the recipe sheet. Put download controls outside the printed sheet. Preserve recipe wording, quantities, temperatures, timing, and storage guidance when transferring existing recipes. Do not introduce another recipe template or decorative food image in place of the printed design.

Existing pages can be converted when they are next revised; this instruction does not authorize changing their recipes.
