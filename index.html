Next family adventure · New Hampshire

Back to Lost River.

The campground that brought the family back three years in a row is calling again. Trail Tater is planning a return to Lost River Valley Campground in North Woodstock, New Hampshire for our 2027 family campout.

📅 July 28 – August 3, 2027   •   📍 North Woodstock, NH

Return trip

Lost River Valley Campground, round four.

Lost River was our family campground for three consecutive summers from 2021 through 2023. In 2027, the plan is to bring Trail Tater back—with a bigger family crew, a riverside basecamp, plenty of camp cooking, and another week of White Mountain memories.

Planning note: Dates, sites, and attendance are working plans until campground reservations and individual travel plans are finalized.

🏕️

The basecamp

Sites 17, 18 & 27

The preferred cluster puts the family together with a double tent site, a two-room cabin, and a nearby water/electric RV site for Trail Tater.

🌊

Why we return

The river is part of camp.

Lost River gives us mountain camping with the river right there, room to spread out, hammocks, campfires, and enough nearby adventures without having to turn the week into a road rally.

🔥

Family campout

A bigger campfire.

This trip is being planned as a family gathering: tents, cabin, camper, shared meals, coffee, fudge, stories, and whoever ends up claiming the best chair around the fire.

The plan so far

Seven days in the White Mountains.

Wednesday, July 28Arrival day, set up the family basecamp, get the coffee station operational, and remember where everybody packed the tent stakes.
During the weekRiver time, campground wandering, camp cooking, hammocks, family photos, local shops, and whatever spontaneous detours earn a Trail Tater story.
Must-do returnClark's Bears is already on the list, along with some White Mountain exploring and plenty of time that does not require a schedule.
Tuesday, August 3Break camp, one last look at the river, and begin discussing the trip we should probably take next.
