New look, same Trail Tater

A fresh banner. A new channel. Same campfire.

We gave Trail Tater a little new paint: a new YouTube banner and profile mark built around the camper, the mountains, and the campfire. The website and channel are growing together, but the idea behind them has not changed.

“The big difference between me and a content creator is they want followers. I want friends.”

This is not a polished travel show. Things go right, things go sideways, somebody forgets something, and eventually we end up back around the fire.
