Day 1 – Arrival in Plymouth

Wednesday, July 29, 2026

We rolled into Sandy Pond Campground at approximately 2:10 PM after a drive that featured wave after wave of heavy rain. As we drew closer to Plymouth the skies finally gave us a break, allowing us to get Trail Tater parked, leveled, and settled into camp.

Once the camper was ready, the tents went up, chairs gathered around the fire ring, and Starlink was connected so this could become the first live Trail Tater journal entry posted from the road.

Weather

Overcast with a light breeze during setup. Showers remain possible overnight with temperatures in the mid-60s.

Camp Menu

  • Dinner: Burgers & Hot Dogs 🍔🌭
  • Breakfast: Pancakes, Bacon, Sausage & Maple Syrup 🥞🥓🍁

After camp was complete, we headed into Plymouth in search of food… just like our forefathers did when they first arrived—only we had GPS.
