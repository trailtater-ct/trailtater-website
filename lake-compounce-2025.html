Every camper needs a first trip. Trail Tater’s began with a fall weekend in Connecticut, a front-row campsite, a Halloween event and two neighboring bulldogs who stole the only surviving photograph.

The first weekend

The trip included Ken, Lisa, Billie and Billie’s boyfriend. It was a practical shakedown weekend—close enough to home to learn the new camper without turning every forgotten item into a crisis, but still a real family getaway.

Lake Compounce gave Trail Tater its first chance to become more than a camper sitting in the driveway. Over the course of the weekend, the family learned how the space worked, where things belonged and what would make future trips easier.

Front row and easy access

The pull-through RV site had water and electric and sat in the front row near the campground office, store, bathrooms and showers. For a first outing, that convenient location made setup and campground life pleasantly simple.

— The first chapter

Phantom Fall Fest

Billie and her boyfriend headed into Lake Compounce for Phantom Fall Fest, the park’s Halloween event, while Ken and Lisa had time back at the campground. It gave the weekend a perfect October personality—part first camping trip, part fall getaway and part spooky-season adventure.

The owners of the campsite next door

The most memorable photograph from the whole weekend was not of the camper or the amusement park. It was of the two bulldogs next door, one standing proudly on the picnic table while the other watched from below.
