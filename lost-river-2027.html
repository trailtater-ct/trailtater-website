A family favorite, revisited

Some places don’t need an introduction. They need a reunion.

Lost River was our wooded summer home for three consecutive years. In 2027, we’ll return with more family, more sleeping room, and the same simple plan: cook together, explore the White Mountains, listen to the river, and let the best parts happen between the plans.

Three sites · one camp

Our little neighborhood in the woods.

Each space has a job, but camp belongs to everyone. Site 27 keeps the coffee hot, Cabin 18 offers four walls and a porch, and Double Tent Site 17 brings us back to familiar ground.

The good stuff

Camp life is the main event.

Breakfast starts with the hiss of the coffee pot at Site 27. The day unfolds through river walks, campground wandering, card games, and somebody asking what’s for dinner. At night, every path leads back to the same glow.

Always running through the story

The river is part of camp.

Lost River is more than scenery. It is the sound behind conversation, cool air under the trees, stones to study, and a reason to slow down. We’re coming back to see what changed—and what feels exactly the same.

“The camper changed. The family grew. The reasons for going never did.”
