Lost River Mountain Breakfast

A hearty breakfast for a Lost River morning — everybody builds the plate they want. No two plates have to be the same.

Build your breakfast

  • Eggs: fried, scrambled, over easy, omelet, or Sade’s World Famous Banana Omelet
  • Breakfast meat: bacon, sausage, or a little of both
  • Mountain sides: home fries / camp potatoes, pancakes, or toast
  • Trail Tater Percolated Camp Coffee

The Lost River Plate

  1. Start with eggs made your way.
  2. Add bacon or sausage.
  3. Pile on some home fries or camp potatoes.
  4. Choose pancakes or toast on the side.
  5. Finish it off with a hot mug of Trail Tater Percolated Camp Coffee.

Options

  • Mix and match sides instead of choosing only one.
  • Set coffee-bar fixings out separately so everyone finishes their own mug.
Trail Tater rule: choices are part of the recipe. The whole point is that everybody can build the breakfast they actually want.
