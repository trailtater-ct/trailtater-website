Meet the Trail Tater Crew

Family. Camping. Road trips. History. Food. And usually one more stop than we planned.

Trail Tater is really just our family doing what we’ve always done — getting out, going somewhere, trying something new, eating well, and collecting stories along the way.

Some trips are carefully planned. Some take a few unexpected turns. Some involve historic sites, campgrounds, roadside attractions, ridiculous photographs, or meals that somehow require three different cooking appliances.

That’s part of the fun.

Welcome to the crew.

Campfire Ken

Ken is the planner, driver, outdoor cook, campground researcher, history guy, gear collector, and the person most likely to turn a simple weekend camping trip into a full-blown expedition.

He handles much of the planning behind Trail Tater — finding campgrounds, researching places to visit, building menus, testing camping gear, and figuring out how much stuff can reasonably fit into one camper.

He also firmly believes that camp food should be considerably better than hot dogs every night.

Lisa

Lisa is part of the heart of Trail Tater and has been along for years of family trips, campgrounds, road trips, historic stops, and all the memories that eventually became this website.

She also provides an important counterbalance whenever Campfire Ken begins a sentence with:

“You know what else we could bring...”

Billie

Billie grew up camping, traveling, hiking, visiting historic places, and being dragged into family photographs whether she particularly wanted one at that moment or not.

She brings plenty of outdoor knowledge of her own to Trail Tater adventures.

Billie also has no shortage of opinions about where we should go, what we should do when we get there, and which photographs absolutely should never appear on the internet.

Her official Trail Tater nickname remains a matter of negotiation.

Stewie

Campground Security & Snack Inspection Department

Stewie is the four-legged member of the Trail Tater crew.

His responsibilities include campsite patrol, investigating unfamiliar smells, testing sleeping surfaces, supervising meal preparation, and determining whether unattended food has legally become his property.

He takes his position very seriously.

The wider circle

The Adventure Crew

Trail Tater has never really been limited to the four of us.

Over the years, nieces, nephews, cousins, friends, and assorted family members have joined the adventures.

Some come for one trip.

Some keep coming back.

And some apparently get recruited whether they realized they were joining the Trail Tater crew or not.

The Adventure Crew changes a little from trip to trip, and that's exactly how we like it.

Gettysburg

Our Friend Abe

Yes, that's Abraham Lincoln in the family picture.

Ken has always had a particular respect for Lincoln — not because of the statues or the mythology surrounding him, but because of the man himself: a husband and father dealing with ordinary family struggles while carrying the extraordinary responsibility of preserving the Union and confronting slavery during the Civil War.

So when the opportunity comes along to get a family picture with our friend Abe, we're taking it.

Every time.
