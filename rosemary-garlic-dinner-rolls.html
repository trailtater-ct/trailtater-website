Rosemary-Garlic Dinner Rolls

Soft, buttery rolls for the holiday table, brushed with garlic butter.

Makes: 12 rollsOven: 375°FTime: 2 1/2-3 1/2 hr

Roll Dough

  • 3 1/4-3 1/2 cups all-purpose flour (390-420 g), divided
  • 2 1/4 tsp instant yeast (1 packet)
  • 2 Tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup milk, lukewarm (100-110 F)
  • 1 large egg, room temperature
  • 1/4 cup butter, softened

Herbs / Garlic Butter

  • 1 Tbsp finely chopped fresh rosemary, or 1 tsp dried, crushed
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 2 small garlic cloves, finely minced
  • Pinch of salt, optional

Trail Tater Method

  1. Mix 3 1/4 cups flour with yeast, sugar, salt, rosemary and garlic powder. Add milk, egg and softened butter. Knead 8-10 minutes by hand (5-7 in a mixer), adding remaining flour only as needed for a soft, slightly tacky dough.
  2. Place dough in a greased bowl, cover and rise until doubled, about 60-90 minutes. Grease a 9 x 13-inch baking pan.
  3. Divide into 12 equal pieces. Pull edges of each piece underneath and pinch closed to form a smooth ball. Arrange seam down in 3 rows of 4.
  4. Cover and rise 35-60 minutes, until puffy and nearly touching. Heat oven to 375 F. Bake 18-23 minutes, until golden and the center of a middle roll reaches 190 F.
  5. While rolls bake, melt 2 Tbsp butter in a small pan over low heat. Add minced garlic; cook gently 1-2 minutes without browning. Add optional salt. Brush hot rolls with garlic butter; rest 10 minutes before serving.

Make Ahead

After shaping, cover tightly and refrigerate overnight (up to 12 hours). Before baking, let stand covered at room temperature until puffy, usually 1-2 hours. Bake as directed.
