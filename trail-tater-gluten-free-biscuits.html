Trail Tater Gluten-Free Biscuits

An easy camp-oven biscuit so the gluten-free Scout or camper doesn’t have to watch everybody else eat biscuits.

About 8 biscuits425°FPropane camp oven

Ingredients

  • 2 cups 1-to-1 gluten-free baking flour blend that includes xanthan gum
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 6 tablespoons cold butter, cut into small pieces
  • 3/4 cup cold buttermilk, plus a little extra if needed
  • Optional: 1/2 to 3/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • Optional topping: melted butter

Trail Tater Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a small baking pan with parchment paper.
  2. Whisk together the gluten-free flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt.
  3. Cut in the cold butter until the mixture looks crumbly with a few pea-size pieces remaining.
  4. Stir in the cheddar now if using.
  5. Add the cold buttermilk and stir just until a soft dough forms. If dry or crumbly, add buttermilk 1 tablespoon at a time.
  6. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface and gently pat it to about 3/4 inch thick. Fold it over once or twice for a little layering, but do not overwork it.
  7. Cut biscuits with a 2- to 2 1/2-inch cutter. Press straight down instead of twisting.
  8. Place biscuits close together on the pan and bake about 13–17 minutes, until lightly golden and set in the centers.
  9. Brush with melted butter if desired and serve warm.
Camp-oven note: small propane ovens can have hot spots. Preheat fully, keep the pan near the center, rotate once during baking, and start checking at about 12 minutes.
